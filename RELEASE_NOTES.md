# SideQuest v1.0.20 — Ticket price fix 🎟️

A quick but important fix.

## 💰 Real prices everywhere
Ticketed events sometimes showed **"from R2 150"** in the event line but
**"💰 FREE"** in the stats bar below — confusing when you're planning a night
out. Now the price shown matches the ticket price everywhere: on feed cards
and in the quest sheet. No more "FREE" on a R2k event.
