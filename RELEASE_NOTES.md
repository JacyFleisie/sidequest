# SideQuest v1.0.17 — The Golden Hour release 🌅

This one's a big one. We rebuilt how the app looks and feels, added squads, and made every quest a little more real.

## ✨ A whole new look
- **Golden Hour redesign** — warm sand and sunset amber instead of the old dark theme. It should feel like a South African afternoon now, not a gaming lobby.
- Home greets you by name — *"Molo, you 👋"*
- New icon system and calmer typography throughout.

## 🛡️ Squads
- Create a squad, invite friends, and see the roster update live.
- Leave or disband whenever you want — and while you're in a squad, every quest you finish earns **+20% XP**.

## ⏳ Seasonal quests
- Limited-time quests tied to real SA events — Braai Day, Comrades, Splashy Fen, the National Arts Festival and more.
- Each one shows an "Ends Sunday" countdown and disappears when the event passes. Catch them while you can.

## ⭐ Real reviews
- Finished a quest? Now you can rate it — real stars from people who actually went.
- Reviews are only accepted from people who completed the quest, checked against a blocklist, and reportable.
- All the made-up "1,243 people completed this" numbers are gone. Every number you see is real.

## 🛣️ Directions & drive time
- Every quest now shows roughly how long the drive will take, with buttons to jump straight into **Google Maps or Waze** for turn-by-turn.

## 🇿🇦 More of the map
- 60+ new quests across all 9 provinces — Stony Point penguins, the Eye of Kuruman, Sodwana and more. 325+ hand-written quests now, and a data checker so the map only grows cleaner.

## 🧹 Housekeeping
- Full account deletion — your data, gone, whenever you want it.
- A bigger update banner and a cleaner "What's new" sheet.
- Fixed the push-notification registration race so update & friend alerts reach your phone reliably.
- A quiet little BETA tag — we're still building, and we're glad you're questing with us early.

Made with ❤️ and a braai somewhere in South Africa 🇿🇦
